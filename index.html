1024-Point Radix-2 FFT Processor — RTL to GDS

A memory-based FFT that reuses one radix-2 decimation-in-time butterfly across all ten stages: address generator, two 512×32 data SRAM banks, a 512-entry twiddle ROM, the butterfly core and an eleven-state controller FSM. 16-bit fixed point, Q1.15 in, Q9.7 out.

Synthesized in Design Compiler against the 65nm library with SRAM compiler models black-boxed, placed and routed with clock tree synthesis, then signed off in PrimeTime on extracted parasitics with propagated clocks and OCV analysis. Verified at three stages — RTL, post-synthesis netlist and post-layout netlist — against a MATLAB fft() golden reference. Calibre DRC and LVS clean: 560,773 layout instances matched 549,051 source instances with all 178 ports corresponding, metal density violations around the SRAM macros waived per foundry guidance.

ESP-Chiplets — Physical Design of a 2×2 Chiplet SoC

Full HLS-to-GDS flow on Columbia's ESP tile-based platform, covering processor, accelerator, memory, shared-local-memory and auxiliary I/O tiles. Two variants were built under identical constraints — one with four bidirectional inter-chiplet links, one without — for a controlled comparison of the interconnect.

High-level synthesis for the accelerator tiles, Genus synthesis, Innovus place-and-route, Tempus static timing analysis across PVT corners. Integrated ASIC memory macros and I/O cells, adapting the wrappers to the ESP memory protocol and validating through generated testbenches. Switching activity was back-annotated from RTL and gate-level simulation into power analysis, then combined with hop latency and NoC topology to compute energy per bit per hop. Work with Nainika Kandarpa.

Min-Area DFT-s-OFDM Transceiver — Mixed-Radix Transform Engine

One shared butterfly runs both radix-2 and radix-3 to compute DFT12, FFT64 and IFFT64 on the same datapath, folding three transforms onto a single arithmetic core for minimum area. A control FSM decodes commands and back-pressures the input over a ready/valid handshake while the SRAMs are busy.

Ran a thirteen-test regression in Icarus Verilog against Python golden models and foundry SRAM models, covering the OFDM transmit and receive paths end to end. Executed the full RTL-to-GDSII flow in GlobalFoundries 180nm with LibreLane and OpenROAD, with post-route ECOs and incremental re-routing scripted through OpenDB in Tcl and Python.

Full-Custom 8-Bit Microprocessor Core — Schematic to Layout

Transistor-level design of an 8-bit adder, level-sensitive latch, tristate bus driver, 3-to-1 multiplexer, SRAM block, shifter and PLA instruction decoder, clocked on two-phase non-overlapping phi1/phi2 with phi1-stable control over a shared internal bus. Work with Athul Raj M R.

DRC and LVS in Calibre, post-layout functional verification in UltraSim on capacitance-only extracted parasitics, critical-path timing in Spectre. The testbench drives the 8-bit bus from two ADCs against a 6-bit instruction set, holding the tristate buffer at Hi-Z to avoid contention; load/store, shift and ADD writeback all verified. A companion study on TSMC N16 FinFET covered FO4 delay estimation, subthreshold-slope extraction at 0.8 V and a DRC/LVS-clean four-finned inverter layout.

64-Tap Fixed-Point FIR Filter with Clock Domain Crossing

A dual-clock FIFO carries 10 kS/s samples from a 10 kHz input domain into an 800 kHz core domain. Coefficient memory in Q1.15 feeds a resource-shared MAC — one multiplier, one adder, stepped sequentially across taps to hold area down — alongside a register file and a five-state controller FSM.

Inputs and coefficients in Q1.15, output in Q7.9, with wider intermediates to limit rounding error. Verified with built-in self-test, randomized input and coefficient vectors, and a MATLAB signature check; pre- and post-synthesis waveforms matched. Synthesized in Design Compiler from Tcl scripts, with setup and hold analysis in PrimeTime.

BERT-Tiny Feed-Forward Accelerator — HLS Design-Space Exploration

A SystemC accelerator for the fully-connected feed-forward layer of BERT, synthesized in Catapult HLS into two Pareto-optimal micro-architectures — SMALL and FAST — from a single source through preprocessor-switched configurations.

Swept target clock period, fixed-point precision, loop unrolling and pipelining, private local memory organization, and DMA width from 32 to 512 bits with varying channel counts. Designs were scored on weighted FPGA resource use (BRAM18/36, LUT, FF, latch, DSP) against effective latency, behind a hard validity gate of at least 70% inference accuracy plus behavioral-to-RTL co-simulation equivalence, then integrated into a complete ESP SoC and prototyped on a VC707.

AHRS Attitude Accelerator — Fixed-Point Kalman Filter in Fabric

A fixed-point Kalman filter in SystemVerilog fuses gyroscope, accelerometer and magnetometer data into roll, pitch and yaw on a Cyclone V. The IMU is modeled in C on the HPS with injected bias, drift and white Gaussian noise, streaming synthetic frames to the fabric over Avalon through a memory-mapped register and FIFO interface.

A VGA rendering engine drives a 640×480 at 60 Hz primary flight display: a horizon line rotating with roll and translating with pitch against a pitch ladder, plus a heading strip with a moving yaw cursor. Post-synthesis timing and RTL issues were debugged down to stable roll and pitch convergence within 30 iterations at 50 MHz. Team of four.
